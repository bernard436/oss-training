Rules for every discovery app

  • One question, a target fixed in advance, a visible counter.
  • One or two taps, from a QR code or a phone link. No login.
  • No link to SAP or LineLog. Data stays on the device that captured it.
  • Download the CSV every day.
  • At the end: export, validation by the owner, then delete the app and the raw data.
  • No names, no individual tracking: figures per line and shift only. The works council is informed before start.
