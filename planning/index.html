In one sentence

The plastic spends about 19 hours in the plant, but SAP and the procedures assume material is always there, lines are interchangeable, a changeover takes one hour and every lot is good; on the floor, late materials, shared equipment, changeovers from 15 minutes to 4 hours and lab holds are handled by phone and in notebooks.
